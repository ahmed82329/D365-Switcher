**D365 Company & Environment Switcher** respects your privacy.

## Information Stored

The extension stores the following configuration data locally in your browser:

- Environment URLs
- Company codes
- Descriptions
- User preferences

This information is used only to provide and personalize the extension's functionality.

## Data Collection

The extension does **not** collect or transmit:

- Personal information
- Analytics or telemetry
- Tracking information
- Browsing activity
- Company or environment data

Your configuration data is not shared or sold. It remains under your control on your device.

## Data Removal

You can remove the stored information by:

- Deleting your saved configurations in the extension, or
- Removing the extension from your browser
